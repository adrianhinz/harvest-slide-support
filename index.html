Common questions

How do I save my progress?
Your progress saves automatically on your device as you play.

Why am I seeing ads?
Harvest Slide is free and supported by ads. Rewarded ads are always optional — you choose when to watch one. Occasional interstitial ads may appear between levels.

How do I remove the ads?
Tap the "No Ads" button in the top bar of the home screen. The one-time Remove Ads purchase removes banner and interstitial ads for good. Rewarded ads stay available only when you choose them (for example, the Hint and X3 buttons).

How do I stop personalized ads?
When the app asks about tracking, choose "Ask App Not to Track", or change it any time in iOS Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking.
